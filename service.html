FAQ

Service questions

Do you service boats you didn't sell?

Yes. We service most makes, with specialist expertise in Mercury and MerCruiser power and the Chaparral, Avalon and Aura boats we sell.

When should I schedule winterization?

As early as possible. Fall fills up fast on the lake. Call the service desk to get on the schedule. Booking winterization with storage gets you priority.

Can you pick up my boat from my dock?

Ask our service desk about pick-up and delivery options around Lake Wallenpaupack.

Are you Mercury certified?

Yes. Our technicians are certified on Mercury and MerCruiser engines.
