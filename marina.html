FAQ

Storage questions

When should I reserve winter storage?

Call us by late summer to reserve. Indoor space on the lake fills early, and storage customers get priority winterization.

How does dry stack launching work?

Our dry stack runs on Speedy Dock. Request a launch in the Speedy Dock app with your arrival time, and our team will have your boat launched and waiting at the dock. Prefer to call or text? That works too.

Can I store a boat I didn't buy from Capri Marine?

Yes, subject to space. Ask our team about availability for your boat's length.
